## About the Book

*Accountable AI at Scale: Fueling Fast and Effective AI Deployment* (co-authored with Radha Iyengar Plumb) is forthcoming from Wiley in February 2027.

Plumb is a former Pentagon Chief AI Officer. Kevin directs Wharton's Accountable AI Lab. Their argument: accountability is what lets organizations deploy AI fast. Companies that build it into their operations ship sooner, take on less risk, and keep the trust of customers, regulators, and employees.

The book draws on AI deployments across government and Fortune 500 companies, including failures that cost billions and shaped regulatory policy. It lays out the processes, organizational structures, and cultural norms that turn AI ethics principles into governance you can measure and run.

## What's Inside

Sixteen chapters in four parts:

- Part I, The Essentials: AI and accountability basics, the business case, and risk assessment as a decision-making tool
- Part II, The Six Pillars: accuracy, fairness, transparency, misuse, privacy and data protection, and intellectual property
- Part III, The Consequences: liability, regulation, societal impacts, and political, market, existential, and environmental risk
- Part IV, Implementation and Beyond: fitting governance to your context, organizational design, and tools and techniques for getting it done

An appendix provides an AI risk assessment worksheet.

## Who It's For

Executives, product managers, engineers, data scientists, and policymakers responsible for AI systems with real consequences.
